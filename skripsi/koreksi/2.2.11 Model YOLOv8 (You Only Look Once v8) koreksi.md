## 1. Masalah pada Draf Terdahulu

Pada draf naskah terdahulu, ditemukan sejumlah hal yang perlu dikoreksi dan disempurnakan:
1. **Atribusi Metrik dan Latensi Komputasi Bergerak yang Kurang Presisi**:
   - Draf lama mencantumkan klaim *"efisiensi waktu inferensi (~3,2 ms per citra pada CPU seluler)"* yang diatribusikan ke `(Khan et al., 2023)`. Faktanya, artikel *Khan et al. (2023)* menguji implementasi seluler Android berbasis varian *nano* (YOLOv8n) pada tanaman dan membandingkannya terhadap YOLO generasi sebelumnya, sedangkan karakteristik modularitas skala YOLOv8 (*nano, small, medium, large, extra-large*) dipaparkan oleh [Terven et al. (2023)](file:///d:/backup/proyek-kerja/proyek%20ku/skripsi/skripsi/jurnal%20referensi/Terven%20et%20al.%20%282023%29.md) dan [Siahaan et al. (2024)](file:///d:/backup/proyek-kerja/proyek%20ku/skripsi/skripsi/jurnal%20referensi/Siahaan%20et%20al.%20%282024%29.md). Narasi telah diselaraskan agar tidak memuat angka klaim yang tidak tertera langsung pada teks jurnal.
2. **Kekeliruan Penulisan / Tipografi Visual**:
   - Terdapat *typo* pada baris penutup diagram arsitektur lama: `Gambar 2.1 Diagram Arsitektur Jaringan Klasifikasi YOLOv8 (yolov8s-cls)s` (terdapat huruf "s" berlebih di akhir kata).
3. **Penyempurnaan Alur Pengekstraksi Fitur**:
   - Draf lama belum menguraikan mekanisme perangkaian (*concatenation*) luaran antar-blok *bottleneck* dalam modul C2f yang diungkapkan oleh [Khan et al. (2023)](file:///d:/backup/proyek-kerja/proyek%20ku/skripsi/skripsi/jurnal%20referensi/Khan%20et%20al.%20%282023%29.md) dan [Terven et al. (2023)](file:///d:/backup/proyek-kerja/proyek%20ku/skripsi/skripsi/jurnal%20referensi/Terven%20et%20al.%20%282023%29.md), serta integrasi normalisasi tumpukan (*batch normalization*) dengan aktivasi *Sigmoid Linear Unit* (SiLU).

## 3. Bukti Transkrip Teks Asli dari Jurnal Referensi

### A. [Terven et al. (2023)](file:///d:/backup/proyek-kerja/proyek%20ku/skripsi/skripsi/jurnal%20referensi/Terven%20et%20al.%20%282023%29.md) (Halaman 1701–1702 / Baris 902–927)
> *"YOLOv8 was released in January 2023 by Ultralytics, the company that developed YOLOv5. YOLOv8 provided five scaled versions: YOLOv8n (nano), YOLOv8s (small), YOLOv8m (medium), YOLOv8l (large) and YOLOv8x (extra-large). YOLOv8 supports multiple vision tasks such as object detection, segmentation, pose estimation, tracking, and classification.*
> 
> *YOLOv8 Architecture: Figure 17 shows the detailed architecture of YOLOv8. YOLOv8 uses a similar backbone as YOLOv5 with some changes on the CSPLayer, now called the C2f module. The C2f module (cross-stage partial bottleneck with two convolutions) combines high-level features with contextual information to improve detection accuracy... It uses the softmax function for the class probabilities, representing the objects' probabilities belonging to each possible class... Figure 17. YOLOv8 architecture. The architecture uses a modified CSPDarknet53 backbone. The C2f module replaces the CSPLayer used in YOLOv5. A spatial pyramid pooling fast (SPPF) layer accelerates computation by pooling features into a fixed-size map. Each convolution has batch normalization and SiLU activation."*

### B. [Siahaan et al. (2024)](file:///d:/backup/proyek-kerja/proyek%20ku/skripsi/skripsi/jurnal%20referensi/Siahaan%20et%20al.%20%282024%29.md) (Halaman 2494–2495 / Baris 154–164)
> *"The YOLOv8 algorithm architecture consists of three main parts, namely: Backbone, Neck, and Head, as illustrated in Figure 3. The YOLOv8 backbone maintains a similar structure to YOLOv5, with the C2f module, based on the Cross Stage Partial (CSP) concept and the idea of ELAN in YOLOv7, providing a richer gradient flow and improving the model's ability to capture important image features. The Spatial Pyramid Pooling Fast (SPPF) module at the end of the backbone ensures high-accuracy object detection at various scales. The combination of C2f and SPPF modules enables YOLOv8 to maintain a lightweight structure, suitable for real-time applications that require fast inference without sacrificing accuracy."*

### C. [Khan et al. (2023)](file:///d:/backup/proyek-kerja/proyek%20ku/skripsi/skripsi/jurnal%20referensi/Khan%20et%20al.%20%282023%29.md) (Halaman 6 / Baris 402–411)
> *"The change in the structure of YOLOv8 is that the c3 module is replaced with the c2f module. In module c2f, outputs from Bottleneck are concatenated, although the output of the last Bottleneck was used in the c3 module. Also, the first 6x6 convolutional layers are replaced with a 3x3 convolutional block in the Backbone module. The main thought behind using the YOLOv8 model was the performance and accuracy boosts during training and inferencing. It is better than the previous versions of YOLO in all aspects (mAP, latency, speed, FPS, and size)."*
