## 1. Masalah pada Draf Lama

Pada naskah draf awal, terdapat beberapa kekeliruan fatal dan ketidaksesuaian sitasi dengan teks asli jurnal:
1. **Ketidaksinkronan Penomoran Judul**: Judul tertulis `2.2.4 Penyakit pada Buah Kakao`, tidak runtut dengan sub-bab sebelumnya (`2.1.1`, `2.1.2`, dan `2.1.3`).
2. **Pencampuradukan Gejala Penyakit & Manipulasi Sitasi pada Black Pod**: Draf lama menyebutkan *"menembus lapisan endokarp... mengeluarkan serbuk spora keputihan di permukaan kulit buah"* dan menyitirnya ke `Ferraris et al. (2023)`. Faktanya, artikel *Ferraris et al. (2023)* tidak pernah membahas lapisan endokarp atau serbuk spora keputihan. Ciri "serbuk spora keputihan" (*white powder known as spores*) sebenarnya adalah ciri infeksi jamur *Moniliophthora roreri* (*Frosty Pod Rot*) pada *Alvarado et al. (2025)* baris 354, bukan ciri patologi *Black Pod*. Draf lama keliru mencampuradukkan gejala jamur Monilia ke Black Pod.
3. **Klaim Biologis Fiktif pada Serangan PBK**: Draf lama memuat rincian entomologi *"memakan jaringan plasenta pembungkus biji serta saluran hara... biji kakao saling melekat satu sama lain dan melekat kuat pada kulit bagian dalam (clumping), berukuran pipih, keras, dan sulit dikeluarkan saat panen"* yang diatribusikan ke `Ariawan et al. (2025)` dan `Maylianti et al. (2025)`. Faktanya, kedua artikel tersebut adalah riset sains data dan visi komputer (DenseNet-121, MobileNetV2, EfficientNet-B0, ResNet-50) yang tidak pernah memuat istilah plasenta, saluran hara, atau *clumping*.

### A. [Alvarado et al. (2025)](file:///d:/backup/proyek-kerja/proyek%20ku/skripsi/skripsi/jurnal%20referensi/Alvarado%20et%20al.%20%282025%29.md) (Halaman 9 / Baris 367–377)
> *"Black pod disease (BPD), caused by the Phytophthora palmivora, is a similar fungal disease that can affect the cocoa fruit. Like FPR, BPD causes losses of up to 20–30% of the annual cocoa production in regions such as Latin America, Africa, and Asia [7,32,33]. BPD thrives in tropical regions where the humidity is up to 100% and the temperature is up to 25 °C. See Table 5. However, unlike FPR, BPD not only affects the cocoa fruit but also other parts of the tree such as leaves, branches, stems, and roots. The disease starts with a small wound on the surface of the fruit, which gradually spreads throughout the fruit, turning it brown [32,35]. Figure 7 shows two examples of BPD. Example one shows the disease spreading from the stem. In contrast, example two shows the disease spreading from the apex."*

### B. [Ferraris et al. (2023)](file:///d:/backup/proyek-kerja/proyek%20ku/skripsi/skripsi/jurnal%20referensi/Ferraris%20et%20al.%20%282023%29.md) (Halaman 9 / Baris 421–428 & 446–449)
> *"Phytophthora and Moniliophthora are the main causal agents among cocoa diseases, causing up to 38% of the global cocoa harvest loss [46,47,81]... The diseases caused by Phytophthora manifest themselves in the form of black pod rot, stem canker, leaf, and nursery blights... identifying healthy and damaged beans affected by Moniliophthora and Phytophthora diseases, in three different classes (respectively, called Healthy, Monilla, and Fito)."*

### C. [Ariawan et al. (2025)](file:///d:/backup/proyek-kerja/proyek%20ku/skripsi/skripsi/jurnal%20referensi/Ariawan%20et%20al.%20%282025%29.md) (Halaman 29–30 / Baris 24–25 & 46–54)
> *"categorized into four classes: fruit rot, fruit-sucking pests, pod borers, and healthy fruits... cacao production is often affected by various plant diseases and pests, leading to yield losses of up to 50–80% [3], [4]. One of the most severe diseases affecting cacao production is cacao pod disease, which directly reduces crop yield [5]. There are three primary diseases that impact cacao pods. The first is fruit rot caused by the Phytophthora palmivora fungus. Additionally, fruit-sucking pests (Helopeltis) and pod borers (Conopomorpha cramerella) also pose significant threats, reducing both the quality and quantity of cacao production [6]."*
