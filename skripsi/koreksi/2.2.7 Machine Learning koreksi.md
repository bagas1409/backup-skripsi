## 1. Masalah pada Draf Terdahulu

Pada draf skripsi terdahulu (di berkas referensi lama), terdapat beberapa kelemahan fundamental:
1. **Objek Komoditas Tidak Sesuai (Kopi vs Kakao)**: Draf lama merujuk pada penyakit daun kopi (*karat daun, phoma, cercospora*) dan menyitir literatur lama di luar repositori rujukan (*Li dkk. 2021*).
2. **Ketiadaan Kerangka Alur Kerja Ilmiah**: Draf lama belum mengadopsi struktur tahapan rekayasa *machine learning* (*pipeline knowledge discovery*) yang terstandarisasi untuk pemrosesan citra pertanian dari sumber jurnal internasional.
3. **Belum Mengintegrasikan Batasan Paradigma Konvensional vs Modern**: Draf lama belum menjelaskan keterbatasan empiris metode *machine learning* konvensional (HOG/LBP + SVM) yang menghasilkan akurasi rendah (75%) pada kakao sebagai justifikasi ilmiah perlunya transisi ke *deep learning*.

## 3. Bukti Transkrip Teks Asli dari Jurnal Referensi

### A. [Alvarado et al. (2025)](file:///d:/backup/proyek-kerja/proyek%20ku/skripsi/skripsi/jurnal%20referensi/Alvarado%20et%20al.%20%282025%29.md) (Halaman 2–3 / Baris 91–105)
> *"Based in Machine Learning algorithms (MLA) and Deep Learning algorithms (DLA), CV has become a tool that allows farmers to make better decisions and is also used to identify and classify plant diseases automatically [12,13]. Thus, CV application is a technology that offers in a quick and accurate way the identification of plant diseases in agriculture.*
> 
> *To perform MLA, a large amount of image data are required to be trained and learn the patterns of data to perform a particular function. MLA are classified into supervised and unsupervised learning. In supervised learning, the training data include the output desire or the target, called labels. For example, in [14], an open-source Kaggle dataset was used to identify healthy and damaged cocoa fruits affected by Moniliophthora and Phytophthora diseases. Thus, MLA was developed to detect healthy and unhealthy stages of cocoa fruits. Classification is a common task in supervised learning algorithms. On the other hand, in unsupervised learning, the data are not labeled and the system tries to learn to acquire patterns without prior knowledge."*
